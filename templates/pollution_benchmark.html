4-Stage Industrial Lifecycle & Environmental Fingerprint
How pollution figures evolve through industrial maturation and induce perimeter land proximity drag
Empirical Theoretical Framework
Phase 1 (0–5 yrs) Greenfield / Setup
Announcement & Inception
  • CEPI Score: < 38.0 (Normal)
  • Red Category: < 15% (Clean/Civil)
  • PM2.5: 42–55 μg/m³ (Ambient background)
  • Effluent: Minimal domestic load; CETP unneeded
Real Estate Drag: No negative proximity discount; speculative anticipation.
Phase 2 (5–12 yrs) Anchor Operations
Anchor Growth & Point Sources
  • CEPI Score: 40.0–55.0 (Moderate)
  • Red Category: 20–35% (Assembly/Auto)
  • PM2.5: 60–72 μg/m³ (Stack emissions)
  • Effluent: Internal primary ETP operational
Sweet-Spot Surge: +14.8% price appreciation in 3–8km residential commuter belt.
Phase 3 (12–25 yrs) Ancillary Density
Ancillary Saturation & Strain
  • CEPI Score: 60.0–75.0 (SPA / CPA)
  • Red Category: 40–65% (Dyeing/Forging)
  • PM2.5: 80–98 μg/m³ (High particulate)
  • Effluent: CETP near hydraulic saturation
Proximity Drag Emerges: -3.2% discount at <0.5km; peak divergence with +24.1% 3–8km belt.
Phase 4 (>25 yrs) Remediation / Moratorium
Mature Saturation / Remediation
  • CEPI Score: ≥ 70.0 (CPA) or < 60 (ZLD)
  • Red Category: > 65% (Heavy Chemical)
  • PM2.5: 95–130 μg/m³ (Chronic haze)
  • Effluent: ZLD retrofit mandated by NGT
Acute Proximity Drag: -6.1% to -9.4% drag at <0.5km; mitigated only by complete ZLD upgrade.
