Acute Stigma Drag (<0.5 km)
Land parcels adjacent to the industrial perimeter suffer an acute -6.1% penalty due to particulate emissions, heavy freight diesel noise, and chemical zone buffers.
Agglomeration Sweet Spot (3–8 km)
Property prices surge to a peak premium of +21.4% between 3km and 8km, capturing supply chain labor demand while escaping direct environmental externalities.
Regional Asymptote (>10 km)
Beyond 10km, land values gradually asymptote back to standard district agricultural-residential baseline rates.
