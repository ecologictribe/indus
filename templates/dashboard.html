The 4-Stage Lifecycle Model: Policy Thrust → Anchor Plant → Ancillary Clustering → Maturation
Read Docs
Phase 1 Years 0–2
Policy & Announcement

Government notifications (GIDC, SEZ). Circle rates revised to signal corridor. Market transactions experience speculative buzz but lag until trunk civil work begins.

Phase 2 Years 3–5
Anchor Commissioning

Major anchor entity (e.g. Tata Motors Sanand, Suzuki Mandal, L&T Hazira) starts commercial production. Trunk infrastructure (substations, expressways) becomes operational.

Phase 3 Years 6–9
Ancillary Clustering

Tier-1/2 component vendors, logistics warehouses, and workforce influx arrive. Housing and rental prices in the 3–8km sweet spot jump rapidly.

Phase 4 Years 10+
Maturation & Stigma

Heavy chemical/effluent emissions and trailer freight cause sharp residential discounting in the immediate perimeter (<0.5km), demanding CETP upgrades.

Jantar Ratio

Statutory Floor Lag: Official Ready Reckoner / Circle Rates (Jantar) systematically lag open-market prices by 18 to 36 months.

During initial speculative surges, the circle-to-market ratio drops to ~72%, and only recovers toward statutory compliance once official state gazette revisions catch up.
